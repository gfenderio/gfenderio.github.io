01

What I build at work.

I work at Kyou, a hobby shop that sells through its own website, Shopee, Tokopedia and TikTok Shop, in its own stores and at events. I look after its stock from the moment it arrives until it is sold, wherever it is sold. The code and internal names stay private.

  1. 01 In the warehouse

    8

    stock locations, every move between them on record

    Warehouse system

    I designed Kyou’s warehouse system and orchestrate how its parts work together. Stock is checked in, counted and reconciled until every gap has a reason. Pre-order stock nobody collected goes back on sale. Every move between locations is recorded and can be traced.

    Web · Android · Go · React · PostgreSQL · Kotlin

  2. 02 Sold online

    4

    storefronts, one system keeping them in line

    Web & marketplace integration

    kyou.id and the three marketplaces each keep their own stock, prices and discounts. I built the system that keeps them in line with what is really on the shelves, down to each Tokopedia warehouse. A monthly report follows sales to the money that reaches the bank.

    Web · Go · React · PostgreSQL

  3. 03 Moved between stores

    1

    shared list for stores and warehouses, where three spreadsheets used to be

    Stock rotation between stores

    Kyou’s stores restock from the warehouses. That used to run on three spreadsheets: slow to open, easy to get wrong and hard to check. Now one screen ranks the items, sets quantities from what is on the racks and sends the shipment. Stores and warehouses work from the same list, and each store checks what arrived against what was sent.

    Web · Go · React

  4. 04 Taken to events

    4

    stages tracked, from packing to the trip home

    Event stock

    Events are where stock records drift the most. Goods leave in boxes, sell at the booth, get restocked during the show and come back. A mismatch there is hard to spot and harder to trace. The system follows every box from packing to the trip home, so the stock on the shelf and the stock on record still agree afterwards.

    Android · Web · Go · Kotlin

LittleJS Jam 3 · 2026

Kite fights on Jakarta rooftops. Cut their line before they cut yours.

I designed the game and directed how it looks. The code, and the scripts that draw every sprite pixel by pixel, were written with Claude Code.
