About.

I spent years leading store and department operations in retail — hitting sales targets, keeping inventory and pricing accurate, and training teams. That taught me exactly where a business loses time, and where software actually helps instead of getting in the way.

Now at Kyou I do that for a living: mapping warehouse, logistics, and e-commerce workflows and rebuilding them with AI-assisted automation to cut manual bottlenecks and human error. I’m a proud “vibe coder” — I lean on AI dev tools to prototype fast, structure backend logic, and ship working products.
